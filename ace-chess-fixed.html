How to play Ace Chess

  1. Click New Game → choose Pass & Play or Play vs Computer (then a difficulty).
  2. Pick a time preset (or type minutes) → Apply Time → Start. Clock begins after the first move.
  3. Move pieces by drag & drop or click from → click to. Legal targets highlight.
  4. Timers switch after each move. When a timer hits 0, the other side wins on time.
  5. Undo/Redo won’t affect draw rules. Flip rotates the board.
  6. Focus hides side panels; ⛶ Fullscreen uses the browser fullscreen.
  7. Use Export PGN / Copy FEN / Load FEN to share or resume positions.
